The coordinator is at {{ include "vamos.coordinatorURL" . }} inside the namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}.
It offers {{ include "vamos.poolPlatforms" . | trim | splitList "\n" | join ", " }}; workers push layers to
{{ include "vamos.cacheRegistry" . }}/{{ .Values.cacheRegistry.repository }} and pull bases through
{{ include "vamos.pullThroughMappings" . | trim | splitList "\n" | join ", " }}.

Check that it answers:

  helm test {{ .Release.Name }} -n {{ .Release.Namespace }}

A client builds with --coordinator, or $VAMOS_COORDINATOR, once it reaches the coordinator; from outside the cluster:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "vamos.fullname" . }}-coordinator 8080:{{ .Values.coordinator.service.port }} &
  vamos build <target> --coordinator http://127.0.0.1:8080

load, save and push --push-from=client read images from the registries by the names above, so a client that
delivers must resolve and reach them; the coordinator tells clients which of them the
{{ include "vamos.fullname" . }}-registries ConfigMap marks insecure, and a client of vamos 0.1.4 or later reads
those over plain HTTP as it does, while an earlier client needs the same entries in its own registries.conf.

There is no authentication: keep the coordinator and the registries on a trusted network.
